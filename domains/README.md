# 📚 Domain Study Guides

One guide per **CCAR-P** exam domain. Each has: **task statements**, **key concepts**, **what you must be able to do**, **resources**, and **self-check questions**.

| # | Domain | Weight | Guide | Studied on |
| - | ------ | :----: | ----- | ---------- |
| 1 | Solution Design & Architecture | **17%** | [01-solution-design-architecture.md](01-solution-design-architecture.md) | Days 2–6 |
| 2 | Claude Models, Prompting & Context Engineering | **13%** | [02-models-prompting-context-engineering.md](02-models-prompting-context-engineering.md) | Days 15–18 |
| 3 | Integration | **19%** | [03-integration.md](03-integration.md) | Days 8–13 |
| 4 | Evaluation, Testing & Optimisation | **16%** | [04-evaluation-testing-optimization.md](04-evaluation-testing-optimization.md) | Days 19–23 |
| 5 | Governance, Safety & Risk Management | **14%** | [05-governance-safety-risk.md](05-governance-safety-risk.md) | Days 24–27 |
| 6 | Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management | **14%** | [06-stakeholder-communication-lifecycle.md](06-stakeholder-communication-lifecycle.md) | Days 29–32 |
| 7 | Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement | **7%** | [07-developer-productivity-operational-enablement.md](07-developer-productivity-operational-enablement.md) | Days 33–34 |

> Weights sum to 100%. **Domains 3 + 1 + 4 are 52% of the exam** — spend your time there. Domain 7 is 7%; two days is enough.
>
> Numbering follows the official exam guide (v1.0, July 2026), *not* difficulty or study order. Study them in the order the [plan](../STUDY-PLAN.md) schedules.

**How to use a guide:** read it, turn the *"You must be able to"* bullets into flashcards, then answer the *self-check* questions from memory. If you can't, re-study that sub-topic and log it.
